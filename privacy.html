Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Kiddoxy ("we", "us") offers free learning games for young children and their families. This policy explains what information is collected when you use our website, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using Kiddoxy you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • No accounts or sign-up. We do not ask for names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos or any other personal information to play our games.
  • Settings saved on your device. Game progress, sound settings and similar preferences are stored in your browser's local storage. This stays on your device; we do not receive it, and you can delete it by clearing your browser data.
  • Technical information. Like most websites, our hosting provider (Cloudflare) and our advertising partners automatically receive basic technical data such as IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited and the time of your visit. We use this only to run, secure and improve the site.
  • Emails you send us. If you contact us, we receive your email address and message and use them only to reply.

2. Advertising and cookies

Kiddoxy is free because it shows ads. We use Google AdSense to display them.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Because Kiddoxy is made for children, we ask Google to treat our pages as child-directed. On these pages Google does not show personalised (interest-based) ads and limits the data it collects, in line with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies at www.aboutads.info.
  • To learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The games will keep working, though ads may be less relevant.

3. Children's privacy

Kiddoxy is designed for children under 13 and their parents. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. There are no chat features, user profiles or places for children to enter personal details. Settings for grown-ups are behind a parental gate. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

4. How we use information

  • To provide the games and remember your settings on your device.
  • To keep the site secure and working properly.
  • To show ads that help keep Kiddoxy free.
  • To answer messages you send us.

We do not sell personal information.

5. Third-party links

Some pages link to other sites, such as our YouTube channel. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their content or practices.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or privacy laws in California and India), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information about you, or to object to how it is used. Since we hold almost no personal data, most requests can be met by clearing your browser data. For anything else, contact us.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

8. Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email info@kiddoxy.com or visit our Contact page.
